= ERP:
ERP is an attempt at creating a module based, incremental, enterprise resource planning and operations software for [International Electronics Concern Nepal](https://iec.com.np). Based on Go-Lang (Echo, Pocketbase/Sqlite) Backend with a Svelte Frontend, ERP is currently a warehouse management, and  managed printing serevice tool that fills in the gaps that are not covered by traditional ERPs and Accounting Software.
